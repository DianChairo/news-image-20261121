A 1024×1024 editorial front-page infographic that sums up Saturday 21 November 2026 in six verified stories. A full-width lead panel covers COP31 in Antalya spilling into overtime after its draft deal stripped out every mention of ending fossil-fuel use; a two-column grid in the middle covers Russia's ballistic-missile strike on Kryvyi Rih, Myanmar rejecting the ICJ's genocide ruling as "unfair", the n0n ransomware leak of JPMorgan, Visa and Mastercard customer data, and Jair Bolsonaro's hospitalisation in Brasilia; a bottom strip closes the page with Jaiswal and Rahul's fightback for India in the Wellington Test. Everything is drawn in pure Pillow shapes and text — no photos, no network calls — in a navy, crimson and gold palette.

`build_image.py` renders the infographic to `entry.png`, and `main.py` copies that file into the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
